Features

Everything You Need.
Nothing You Don't.

A complete, professional toolkit designed for modern hoteliers to automate daily tasks.

Live Floor Chart

See which rooms are vacant, occupied, or cleaning in real-time. One-click check-in/check-out.

Restaurant POS

Digital menu, KOT system, room-service orders. Auto-transfer food bills to guest's room folio.

100% CA-Ready GST

Automated 12% lodging and 5% food GST with SAC codes. Download Excel reports for your CA.

Staff & Payroll

Manage staff, record salaries, generate payslips. Role-based access for owner, manager, receptionist.

P&L Analytics

Beautiful charts showing profit, revenue, expense breakdown, and top-selling food items.

Booking Calendar

Month and week view calendar. See room availability at a glance. Prevent double bookings.

Market Due Tracker

Track outstanding balances. Send automated WhatsApp payment reminders to guests.

Aadhaar QR Scanner

Scan Aadhaar QR code with phone camera. Auto-fill guest name and ID. Fast check-in.

UPI Payment QR

Generate UPI QR codes for GPay/PhonePe/Paytm. Guests pay directly. Auto-mark settled.

Day Close Summary

End-of-day reconciliation. Verify cash drawer, UPI receipts, expenses — one click.

Thermal & A4 Print

Print 58mm/80mm thermal bills instantly. Generate professional A4 tax invoices.

1-Click Backup

Download complete JSON backup anytime. Restore on any device. Never lose data.
